Items are produced after you order. Production and shipping are separate stages; delivery estimates are not guarantees.

02 / RETURNS, REPRINTS & REFUNDS

SOMETHING
NOT RIGHT?

Because products are made to order, Printify does not support ordinary returns or exchanges for choosing the wrong size or color, or changing your mind.

If an item arrives damaged, defective or with a manufacturing error, report it within 30 calendar days of delivery and provide clear photos. Eligible issues qualify for a free reprint or refund under Printify’s policy. Support reviews the issue and required evidence.

Contact support before mailing anything back. This summary does not limit rights you may have under applicable consumer law.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact support immediately. Pop-Up Store orders generally cannot be edited after submission; changes may require cancellation and a new order. Cancellation is not guaranteed once production begins.

What if I entered the wrong address?

Contact support as soon as possible. An incorrect address can affect delivery and refund eligibility. Printify’s Pop-Up guidance states that a returned-to-sender or lost order caused by an invalid customer address is eligible for production-cost reimbursement only, not the full shipping charge.
