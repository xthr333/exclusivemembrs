COMPETITION SYSTEM

NOT A
POPULARITY CONTEST.

01 // SONGS

ORIGINAL ONLY

ONE UNRELEASED ORIGINAL SONG PER MATCHUP, FEATURING BOTH DUO MEMBERS. FOUR SONGS PREPARE A DUO FOR EVERY POSSIBLE ROUND.

02 // SCORING

JUDGES DECIDE

EACH OFFICIAL JUDGE GIVES ONE OVERALL SCORE FROM 1–10. THERE IS NO FAN VOTE.

03 // WILDCARDS

TWO LOSERS ADVANCE

Six Opening Round winners advance automatically. The two highest-scoring losing duos advance as Wildcard 1 and Wildcard 2 using the official judges’ Opening Round scores. Eight Quarterfinalists → four Semifinalists → one Championship matchup. No byes.

04 // TIES

JUDGE DISCUSSION

Tied matchup or wildcard scores are resolved through judge discussion about which song was genuinely better and where the judges lean. No audience popularity vote.
