✦ ABOUT EXM

NOT A

CONTEST.

ExclusiveMembrs is a creative collective bringing together artists, producers and creators through music, events, content and community.

THE FOUNDER / WAYNE J.

IT STARTED
WITH MUSIC.

I started ExclusiveMembrs making music with my friends. We were rapping and recording on our phones with basically no budget. Some of my friends questioned the name at first, but they got what I was trying to build: somewhere we could make things and be ourselves.

That’s what “Not a popularity contest” means to me. You shouldn’t need a big following for somebody to take your work seriously. I built SongWars around that idea: bring an original record, let people hear it, and let the judges talk about the music.

With W3 Studios, I get to tell stories from behind the camera too. Music is where EXM started, but I want it to be a place where people can make films, games, clothes, and things I haven’t even thought of yet. The part I want to keep is how it started: people making things together with what they have.
